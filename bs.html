THE RESET

Overview

Presented at NSC 2026 across 9 roadshows and 3 cities, The Reset captures the realities of macro tightening, AI-driven change, uncertain demand in, and the resulting shifts in growth models, capabilities, and talent strategies among India’s tech SMEs. Join us to examine how leading tech SMEs are responding and what it means for your next phase of growth.

GROWTH RESET

From Scale to Sustainability.

Moving beyond vanity metrics, we provide the blueprint for India’s tech SMEs to transition from aggressive scaling to long-term, profitable growth models built for today's macroeconomic reality.

02 — Capability

CAPABILITY RESET

AI as Core, Not an Add-on.

Rather than treating AI as a peripheral tool, this pillar focuses on re-engineering your operational DNA to integrate intelligence at the core of every product and service you deliver.

03 — Talent

TALENT RESET

Learnability over Headcount.

In an AI-shaped world, agility is the new currency. We explore how to rebuild your talent strategy around the speed of learning rather than just the number of hands.

04 — Positioning

POSITIONING RESET

From Vendor to Strategic Partner.

Redefine your seat at the table and learn how to pivot your market identity from a service vendor to a mission-critical growth partner that global clients can't operate without.
