A Glimpse Into #NSC2025

The Nasscom SME Confluence 2025 will address the core needs of modern SMEs - building global alliances for tech leadership, developing next-gen capabilities, and driving innovation for sustainable growth.

Exclusive highlights include the 3rd SME Inspire Awards, celebrating exceptional achievements, and the launch of the SME Report, delivering takeaways to drive growth.

Join us for an enriching experience of inspiration, innovation, and impactful conversations!

Ahmedabad | March 12, 2025

Next-Gen Tech Leadership & Capability Development

A deep dive into future-ready SME capabilities—embracing AI, IoT, and blockchain to foster tech leadership and drive market agility.

  • Build agile enterprises with emerging tech
  • Upskill your workforce for the AI era
  • Leverage industry-academia collaboration for tech talent

Trivandrum | March 18, 2025

Driving Tech Innovation for Scaling up & Future-Proofing Growth

Explore how SMEs can integrate innovative business models to scale up and grow, enhance customer engagement, and secure their digital future.

  • Aligning business goals with Long-Term Success
  • Expansion strategy for Newer markets & frontiers
  • Leveraging AI for Sustainable Business growth
  • Essence of Brand building & Customer Engagement

Delhi | March 20, 2025

Forging Global Alliances for Tech Leadership

Discover strategies to expand globally by leveraging international partnerships, adopting advanced tech solutions, and building leadership that aligns with global standards.

  • Unlock cross-border partnerships
  • Navigate international market dynamics
  • Learn from SMEs that have scaled globally

Celebrating Achievements Honouring Success

An initiative of the Nasscom SME Council, the Nasscom SME Inspire Awards 2025 celebrates MSME leaders excelling in business growth, innovation, and workplace practices.

In its 3rd edition, the awards feature 25 categories across Micro, Small, and Medium segments, including 7 special awards honoring achievements in HR, Diversity & Inclusion, Skilling, AI, CSR, and Sustainability. Recognizing innovation, 3 awards spotlight SMEs in manufacturing and services for advancements in technology implementation.

Thank You

Thank you to our incredible speakers, delegates, sponsors, and partners for making Nasscom SME Confluence 2025 a grand success!


With 750+ attendees, 70+ speakers, and over 30 sessions, NSC 2025 delivered powerful conversations under the theme ‘Tech-ing the Leap: Charting a Global Future.’


Across Ahmedabad, Trivandrum, and Delhi, the event brought together global voices — from ambassadors and policymakers to tech leaders and SMEs — unpacking insights on technology, trade, talent, and trust while touching on every aspect critical to SME transformation and global readiness.


The journey may have reached its finale, but let's keep ambition to scale, lead, and innovate continuing.
